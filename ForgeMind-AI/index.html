          A simple workflow designed around turning an idea into something buildable.

          About ForgeMind AI
        
                    

        
                    
          A project built around the theme “AI se AI ka Nirman”.
        
                    

        
                    

          
                    
            
                    The idea
                    

            
                    
              ForgeMind AI explores how AI tools can help people move from
              a simple idea to a structured AI-based solution. The focus is
              on making the process easier to understand for students and beginners.
            
                    

            
                    
              The current version includes a working AI Builder connected
              to a Node.js backend and Gemini API, plus six AI utility tools
              with structured AI output and frontend rendering.
